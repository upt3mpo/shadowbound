Character Codex

The Shadowbound

A dark support-oriented RPG character concept whose power scales with nearby life. Strength increases with proximity. So does the cost.

Character Identity

Attrition Through Proximity

The Shadowbound converts nearby life into sustained power. This is not a traditional healer fantasy. It is an RPG character built around pressure, conversion, enemy density, and controlled collateral risk.

Design Goals

What This Character Is Built To Test

Readable Power With Visible Cost

Strong moments should feel immediate and satisfying, while the cost remains clear enough for players to understand why their positioning matters.

Positioning As Consequence

Movement is not only about avoiding danger. Where the character stands changes who benefits, who suffers, and how quickly an encounter resolves.

Build Flexibility

The concept supports aggressive drain, defensive sustain, barrier-focused survival, and safer target-bias variants.

Primary Fantasy

Attrition Caster

Weakens enemies, sustains through conversion, and turns enemy density into momentum.

Core Loop

Drain → Convert → Stabilize

Pull vitality from nearby units, convert it into damage, barrier, resource, or self-sustain, then reposition before the cost spreads too far.

Primary Risk

Collateral Drain

Prolonged proximity can endanger companions, friendly NPCs, or protected objectives, creating tension between power and restraint.

Design Pillars

Core Rules

Power Through Proximity

Strength scales with nearby life, hostile or friendly.

Efficiency Over Mercy

Faster resolution carries higher collateral cost.

Control vs Surrender

Light stabilizes. Shadow accelerates.

Positional Responsibility

Character placement directly affects power, risk, and encounter pacing.

Core Mechanic

Aura of Attrition

The Shadowbound emits a passive aura that drains a small percentage of health from nearby living units over time.

  • Scales with number of nearby units
  • Prioritizes enemies but can include companions, bystanders, or friendly NPCs within range
  • Converts drained vitality into damage, barrier, resource, or self-sustain
Playstyles

Build Directions

Controlled Attrition

Smaller aura radius, stronger enemy targeting, and safer sustain for longer dungeon runs or companion-heavy play.

Veil Reaper

High-output drain build that rewards dense enemy packs, fast clears, and aggressive positioning.

Hollow Saint

Defensive conversion build that turns drained vitality into barrier, recovery, and survival tools.

Player Decisions

Core Tradeoffs

  • Stand close to enemy packs for power, or step away for control
  • End encounters quickly, or preserve long-term safety
  • Use nearby life as fuel, or limit output to avoid collateral damage
  • Build around burst, sustain, barrier, or safer target bias
Design Read

Why This Works

The Shadowbound works because the narrative and mechanics ask the same question: how much safety should be sacrificed for speed? The aura gives the character a clear identity, while target bias, radius, conversion type, and item effects provide practical tuning points for balancing risk, reward, and player agency.

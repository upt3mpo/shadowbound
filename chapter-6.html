Chapter VI

The Rift Over Karthain

The breach came at dawn.

It did not begin with an army. It began with absence.

The eastern horizon dimmed as if something had drawn a curtain across the sky. Light failed before the sun could rise. Sound dulled. Even the wind hesitated.

Then the ground opened.

A rift tore through the fields beyond Karthain, splitting earth and root and stone in a line that ran farther than the eye could follow. From within it came movement without shape, pressure without form, a tide of presence that had no need for bodies.

The Husks that followed were only vessels. What truly advanced could not be seen, only felt in the collapse of everything it touched.

The city did not have time to prepare.

Barricades were raised where they could be. Civilians crowded the inner walls. Soldiers formed ranks that had no hope of holding long enough.

They looked to Ilyas.

Not as a priest.

As an answer.

He walked to the front of the line and felt the Veil awaken before he called it.

There was too much life behind him. Too much pressure. Too much warmth waiting to be redirected.

The choice came quickly.

Stand among them, and the Veil would bloom.

Step away, and the line would break.

“If I stand among them, they die before reaching the gate.”

Too many lives in one place turned power into a liability.

No one answered him. They did not understand the rule he was speaking aloud.

Ilyas stepped forward.

Not into the line.

Beyond it.

He walked past the barricades and into the open field where the first wave would break.

The Veil opened fully.

This time, there was no hesitation.

Shadow spread outward in a widening ring, pulling from everything it touched. The Husks slowed, then collapsed, their borrowed movement stripped away. The unseen pressure behind them faltered, disrupted by the sudden absence of momentum.

Behind him, the city held.

No one near the walls collapsed.

Ilyas felt the difference immediately.

Distance changed the equation.

Power diminished.

Control returned.

For the first time since the cathedral, he chose who paid the cost.

The field became quiet.

The rift remained.

Ilyas stood alone between the city and what would come next.

Core Mechanic

Distance as Control

Increasing distance from others reduces total power but isolates cost to enemies, introducing a positional skill ceiling.
